## Convert Text to ALL CAPS

The **Uppercase Converter** instantly transforms every letter in your input into uppercase (capital) letters. 

Commonly referred to as "ALL CAPS," uppercase text is frequently used for emphasis, loud declarations, legal document headings, and normalizing database entries where consistent casing is required (such as postal codes or promotional codes).

### How to Use the Uppercase Converter
1. Type or paste your text into the input box.
2. Select the **UPPERCASE** option from the formatting controls.
3. Click the copy button to save your fully capitalized text to your clipboard.
