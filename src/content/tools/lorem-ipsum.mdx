## What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is standard dummy text used by the typesetting and web design industry. It is used as a placeholder to demonstrate the visual form of a document or a webpage without relying on meaningful content. 

Using placeholder text allows designers to focus entirely on the typography, layout, and visual hierarchy. If real text is used during the design phase, stakeholders often get distracted by reading the content rather than evaluating the design itself.

### Where does it come from?

Contrary to popular belief, Lorem Ipsum is not simply random gibberish. It has roots in a piece of classical Latin literature from 45 BC, making it over 2,000 years old. 

It comes from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of *"de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum"* (The Extremes of Good and Evil) by Marcus Tullius Cicero. The text was altered over centuries by typesetters who mixed up the words and letters to create the nonsensical dummy text we use today.
