## How to Use the Text Cleaner

Our Text Cleaner helps you instantly sanitize messy data, especially text copied from PDFs or poorly formatted emails.

Use the checkboxes above to customize your cleaning:

* **Remove Extra Spaces:** If your text has double or triple spaces between words, this will condense them into a single space.
* **Remove Line Breaks:** This will turn paragraphs into a single continuous block of text by removing all enter/return keystrokes.
* **Remove Tabs:** Strips out all tab characters, which often break formatting when pasting into web forms.
* **Trim Leading/Trailing Spaces:** Removes any invisible spaces at the very beginning or end of lines.

## Why Do I Need to Clean My Text?

When you copy text from complex documents like PDFs, Microsoft Word files, or older websites, hidden formatting characters are often copied to your clipboard alongside the visible words. 

This is particularly frustrating with **PDF files**, which often insert hard line breaks at the end of every visual line. If you paste that text into an email or a modern word processor, the sentences will break unnaturally in the middle of the screen.

Using our Text Cleaner allows you to strip away those hard line breaks and double spaces, restoring your data to pure, readable text.
