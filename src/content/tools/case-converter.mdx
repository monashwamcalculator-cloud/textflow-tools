## How to use the Case Converter

This tool allows you to instantly reformat blocks of text without having to retype them manually. Simply paste your text into the input box and select one of the formatting options above:

* **Sentence case:** Capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence and leaves the rest lowercase.
* **lower case:** Uncapitalizes all letters in the text.
* **UPPER CASE:** Capitalizes every single letter in the text.
* **Title Case:** Capitalizes the first letter of every word (great for headlines or titles).

## Why use this tool?

Whether you accidentally left Caps Lock on while writing a long email, or you need to format a list of titles for a blog post, this tool saves you time and ensures consistent formatting across your documents.
