## How to Use the Word Counter

Simply start typing or paste your document directly into the large text area above. The tool will instantly calculate all metrics in real-time as you type. All processing is done locally in your browser, meaning your text is never sent to an external server.

## Understanding the Metrics

* **Words:** The total number of words in your text. This is useful for school essays, blog posts, or freelance writing assignments.
* **Characters:** The total number of letters, numbers, symbols, and spaces. Most social media platforms (like Twitter/X) restrict post length based on character counts.
* **No Spaces:** The total character count excluding spaces. Often required for specific technical or academic submissions.
* **Sentences:** The number of sentences, calculated by counting punctuation marks like periods, exclamation points, and question marks.
* **Paragraphs:** The number of distinct blocks of text separated by line breaks.
* **Read (Min):** The estimated time it takes a human to read the text. This is based on a standard average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

## Common Social Media Limits (2026)

If you are writing for social media, keep these character limits in mind:
* **Twitter / X:** 280 characters (Standard), up to 10,000 for Premium users.
* **Instagram:** 2,200 characters for captions.
* **LinkedIn:** 3,000 characters for a standard post.
* **YouTube:** 100 characters for a title, 5,000 for a description.
