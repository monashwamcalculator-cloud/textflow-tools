## Format Titles and Headlines Properly

The **Title Case Converter** automatically formats your text so that the first letter of every major word is capitalized, while minor words (like "and", "the", "of", "in") remain lowercase.

This tool is indispensable for journalists, bloggers, students, and content creators who need their headlines, essay titles, and book covers to look professional and adhere to standard publishing guidelines.

### What is Title Case?
In English grammar, Title Case (also known as Headline Style) is the practice of capitalizing the principal words in a title. While different style guides (like APA, Chicago, and AP) have slightly different rules regarding exactly which minor words should be lowercase, the general rule is:
1. Capitalize the first and last word.
2. Capitalize nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs.
3. Lowercase articles (a, an, the), coordinating conjunctions (and, but, for), and short prepositions.
