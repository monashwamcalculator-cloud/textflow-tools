## Clean Up Your Lists Instantly

The **Remove Duplicate Lines** tool scans your text line-by-line and automatically eliminates any repeated entries, leaving you with a perfectly clean list of unique items.

This is an essential utility for marketers cleaning email lists, data analysts preparing spreadsheets, and developers deduplicating arrays or CSV files.

### How to use the Deduplicator
1. Paste your messy list into the input area.
2. The tool instantly processes your text and outputs only unique lines.
3. Toggle "Case Sensitive" if you want `Apple` and `apple` to be treated as unique items rather than duplicates.
